What the format can be used for

Communication & conflict

Rehearse difficult conversations, feedback, disagreement and boundaries in a scene before repeating them at work.

Leadership & change

Make invisible roles and reactions visible, then test different ways of leading, responding or making a decision.

Presence & creativity

Use improvisation to practise pitching, contact, spontaneity and group ideation without turning the session into a talent contest.

A workshop is designed around one real job

Before quoting a program, we clarify who the participants are, what situation matters, what should be different afterwards and what constraints the organization has.
