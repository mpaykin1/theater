Participation is optional

Interactive does not mean that everyone must go on stage. Watching first is a valid way to enter the format.

What can happen in an interactive show?

Recognize a situation

A conflict, a choice, a boundary, a relationship or a repeating life scene becomes visible as action.

Change one move

A participant can try another line, decision or response and see how the scene changes.

Leave with a question

The goal is not a diagnosis or a “correct answer” but a new angle and a possible next action.
