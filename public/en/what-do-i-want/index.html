Three ways to get unstuck before the quiz

Start with what you do not want

Name three things you do not want to keep repeating today. For each, write the smallest opposite action.

Look at envy without judging it

Ask what exactly you envy: freedom, people, courage, movement, attention, creation? It may point toward a missing experience.

Remove “should” for ten minutes

If the next hour did not need to be useful, impressive or correct, what would you try?
